### Game Details & Guide
Cluster Rush: A Comprehensive Guide



Game Overview


C Cluster Rush is a browser-based game developed by Games2Fun, where you play as a bee trying to collect nectar from clusters of flowers while avoiding being chased by other bees.



How to Play



Gameplay:




    

Controls:


    

        
Use the left and right arrow keys to move your bee towards the cluster.

        
Press 'A' to hover over a cluster, revealing its contents.

    





Objective:




    
Collect nectar from clusters without being caught by other bees.

    
Avoid running into other bees and obstacles.





Tips and Tricks for Success



General Tips:




    
Keep an eye out for clusters with multiple nectar drops to maximize your collection.

    
Use the ' hover' feature to spot hidden clusters and avoid danger.

    
Priority clusters should be prioritized, but don't always follow them blindly.






Mouse Control Tips:




    
In tight spaces, use the mouse wheel to increase or decrease your speed.

    
Use the ' zoom' feature (by pressing Ctrl + Plus sign) to see clusters from a distance.
